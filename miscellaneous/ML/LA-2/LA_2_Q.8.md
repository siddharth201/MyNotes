For two vectors to be perpendicular to each other, their dot product must be equal to zero.

Given the vectors:
$\mathbf{a} = [2, 6, 8]$
$\mathbf{b} = [p, 3, 4]$

The dot product of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ is calculated as:
$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = (a_1 \cdot b_1) + (a_2 \cdot b_2) + (a_3 \cdot b_3)$

Substituting the given components:
$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = (2 \cdot p) + (6 \cdot 3) + (8 \cdot 4)$

Since the vectors are perpendicular, their dot product is 0:
$2p + (6 \cdot 3) + (8 \cdot 4) = 0$
$2p + 18 + 32 = 0$
$2p + 50 = 0$

Now, we solve for $p$:
$2p = -50$
$p = \frac{-50}{2}$
$p = -25$
