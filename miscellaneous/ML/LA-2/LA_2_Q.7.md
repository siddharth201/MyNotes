The angle between two vectors can be found using the dot product formula:

$a \cdot b = ||a|| \cdot ||b|| \cdot \cos(\theta)$

where:
*   $a \cdot b$ is the dot product of vectors $a$ and $b$.
*   $||a||$ is the magnitude of vector $a$.
*   $||b||$ is the magnitude of vector $b$.
*   $\theta$ is the angle between vectors $a$ and $b$.

We are given:
*   $||a|| = 1$
*   $||b|| = 2$
*   $a \cdot b = 1$

Let's substitute these values into the formula:
$1 = (1) \cdot (2) \cdot \cos(\theta)$
$1 = 2 \cdot \cos(\theta)$

Now, we can solve for $\cos(\theta)$:
$\cos(\theta) = \frac{1}{2}$

To find the angle $\theta$, we take the inverse cosine (arccosine) of $\frac{1}{2}$:
$\theta = \arccos\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$

The angle whose cosine is $\frac{1}{2}$ is $60^\circ$.
