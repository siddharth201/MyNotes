## Why this is False:
In classification algorithms (like Logistic Regression or Support Vector Machines), the line or hyperplane acts as the decision boundary.

* Near the boundary: The model is uncertain. The probability of belonging to either class is close to 50%.
* Farther from the boundary: The model becomes more confident. The farther a point moves into a specific class's territory, the higher the probability (approaching 100%) that it belongs to that specific class.

Therefore, greater distance from the hyperplane means a higher chance (greater confidence) of belonging to the designated class on that side, not a lower one.
