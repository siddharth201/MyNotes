
## $y_hat = w_1 f_1 + w_2 f_2 + w_3 f3 + w_0$ --> 3D Hyperplane
## is above statement correct?

This statement is technically wrong because the formula describes a 2D plane existing within a 3D space, not a 3D hyperplane.
Here is the breakdown of why this is incorrect:
## 1. Counting the Features (Dimensions)
The equation has 3 independent features: $f_1$, $f_2$, and $f_3$.

* These three features create a 3D ambient space (like a physical room with width, length, and height).

## 2. The Dimension of a Hyperplane
As established earlier, a hyperplane always has one less dimension ($N-1$) than the space it lives in:

* Space ($N$): 3 dimensions ($f_1, f_2, f_3$)
* Hyperplane ($N-1$): $3 - 1 =$ 2 dimensions

## The Correct Terminology
The equation actually defines a standard 2D flat plane slicing through a 3D graph. To make the handwritten note correct, it should say:
$$\rightarrow \text{Hyperplane in 3D space (which is 2D)}$$
