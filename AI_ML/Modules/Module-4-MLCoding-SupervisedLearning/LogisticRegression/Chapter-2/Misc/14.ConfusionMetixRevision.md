# 10. One sentence that I want you to memorize

### FP

> **Model said YES, but reality was NO.**

```text
NO → YES
0  → 1
    ↑
  Model said YES
```

### FN

> **Model said NO, but reality was YES.**

```text
YES → NO
 1  → 0
       ↑
    Model said NO
```

This is much easier than remembering "False Positive" and "False Negative."

---

# 11. Why are FP and FN important?

Because they represent **different types of mistakes**.

### FP — False Positive

```text
Actually: No Disease
Model:    Disease
```

The model creates a **false alarm**.

The person may undergo unnecessary tests/treatment.

---

### FN — False Negative

```text
Actually: Disease
Model:    No Disease
```

The model **misses a disease case**.

This can be much more dangerous.

That's why in medical screening we often care strongly about **Recall**, because Recall tries to reduce FN.

---

# 12. Connect it with Precision and Recall

Now your previous topics become connected.

From the video's numbers:

```text
TP = 45
FP = 25
FN = 5
TN = 125
```

### Precision

Precision asks:

> **"Among people the model called Disease, how many actually had Disease?"**

The model predicted Disease for:

45 + 25 = **70 people**

Correct Disease predictions:

45

So:

**Precision = 45 / (45 + 25)**

= **64.3%**

---

### Recall

Recall asks:

> **"Among all people who actually had Disease, how many did the model find?"**

Actual Disease:

45 + 5 = **50**

Found:

45

So:

**Recall = 45 / (45 + 5)**

= **90%**

This is a beautiful example because you can see:

**Recall = 90%**

but

**Precision = 64.3%**

Why?

The model found almost all disease patients, but it also gave quite a few false alarms.

---

# 13. And this connects to your Threshold lesson

Remember what you just learned:

### Lower threshold

Model becomes more willing to say:

> Disease!

Therefore:

- TP tends to increase
- FN tends to decrease
- Recall tends to increase
- FP may increase
- Precision may decrease

### Higher threshold

Model becomes more strict:

> "I'll only say Disease if I'm very confident."

Therefore:

- FP tends to decrease
- Precision tends to increase
- FN may increase
- Recall may decrease

So all these topics are connected:

```text
Probability
     ↓
Threshold
     ↓
Prediction
     ↓
Confusion Matrix
     ↓
TP / FP / FN / TN
     ↓
Precision / Recall / F1
```

And memorize only these two sentences:

```text

             TP / (TP + FN)

             (TP + TN) / Total

F1        → Balance Precision + Recall
```

### 🎯 Most important takeaway from this lecture

**Don't memorize TP/FP/FN/TN as four independent terms.**

Always do:

**Actual → Predicted → Was it correct? → What did the model predict?**

For example:

**0 → 1**

- Actual = No
- Predicted = Yes
- Wrong
- Model predicted Positive

→ **False Positive**

And:

**1 → 0**

- Actual = Yes
- Predicted = No
- Wrong
- Model predicted Negative

→ **False Negative**

Once this becomes automatic, **Precision, Recall, F1, threshold trade-offs, and even ROC/PR curves will become much easier.**
