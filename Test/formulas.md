
### 1. Percentages, Ratios, Profit & Loss

* **Profit on a single ratio component:**

$$\text{Marked Price} = \text{Total Cost} \times \left(1 + \frac{r_{\text{target}}}{R_{\text{total}}} \times \frac{P}{100}\right)$$


* **Markup ($+x\%$) followed by Discount ($-y\%$):**

$$\text{Net Gain/Loss \%} = x - y - \frac{xy}{100}$$


* **Successive Percentage Growth ($+x\%$, then $+y\%$):**

$$\text{Final Value} = \text{Initial Value} \times \left(1 + \frac{x}{100}\right) \times \left(1 + \frac{y}{100}\right)$$


* **Relative Percentage Less (if $A$ is $R\%$ more than $B$):**

$$\% \text{ Less} = \frac{R}{100 + R} \times 100$$


* **Remaining-of-Remaining (Salary/Savings):**

$$\text{Final Remaining} = \text{Total} \times \left(1 - \frac{a}{100}\right) \times \left(1 - \frac{b}{100}\right)$$


* **Subgroup Percentage (assume Total $= 100$):**

$$\text{Required \%} = \frac{\text{Target Subgroup}}{\text{Total Reference Subgroup}} \times 100$$

### 2. Coding-Decoding

* **Binary Place Values (right to left):**

$$\text{Decimal Value} = \sum (\text{bit} \times 2^n) \quad \text{using weights } \dots, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1$$


* **Alphabetical Position (`EJOTY` Anchor):**
`E = 5`, `J = 10`, `O = 15`, `T = 20`, `Y = 25` (count forward/backward from the nearest anchor).
* **Letter Shift:**

$$\text{Coded Position} = \text{Original Position} \pm k$$


* **Direct Substitution:** Match each letter 1-to-1 with its digit in the given example words; verify using any repeated letter.

### 3. Non-Verbal Reasoning (Visual Matrices)

Check these four rules in order across rows (left $\rightarrow$ right) and columns (top $\rightarrow$ bottom):

1. **Element Count:** Adding or removing a fixed number of lines/shapes per step.
2. **Rotation:** Fixed $45^\circ$, $90^\circ$, or $180^\circ$ clockwise/counter-clockwise turns.
3. **Position Shift:** Moving 1 column/row or 1 step along the perimeter.
4. **Fill Alternation:** Alternating Black / White / Hatched patterns.

---

### 4. Verbal & Clerical Comparison ("Same or Different")

* **Conceptual Pairs:** Check for exact equivalence (abbreviations, capitals, synonyms).
* **String Pairs:** Scan character-by-character for **3 traps**: extra/missing letters (`Johnson` vs. `Johnsons`), single-letter swaps (`Campagnolo` vs. `Campagnalo`), and capitalization (`to` vs. `To`).

### 5. Data Sufficiency

* **Evaluation Order:** Test **Statement I alone** $\rightarrow$ Test **Statement II alone** $\rightarrow$ Combine **I + II** only if neither works alone.
* **Linear Equations Rule:** Two variables ($x, y$) require two **independent** equations ($\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$). If one equation is just a multiple of the other, they are **not sufficient**.
* **Elimination Rule (Ranking):** If $n - 1$ people are ruled out from a position, the remaining $1$ person is uniquely determined.

### 6. Time, Speed, Distance, Trains & Boats/Streams

* **Constant Distance (Inverse Proportion):**

$$S_1 \times T_1 = S_2 \times T_2$$


* **Unit Conversion:**

$$\text{km/hr} \times \frac{5}{18} = \text{m/s} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \text{m/s} \times \frac{18}{5} = \text{km/hr}$$


* **Train Crossing a Pole/Man:**

$$\text{Length of Train} = \text{Speed} \times \text{Time}$$


* **Boats & Streams ($u =$ still-water speed, $v =$ stream speed):**

$$D = u + v, \qquad U = u - v, \qquad u = \frac{D + U}{2}, \qquad v = \frac{D - U}{2}$$


* **Round-Trip Boat Time (distance $d$ each way):**

$$T = \frac{d}{u + v} + \frac{d}{u - v} = \frac{2du}{u^2 - v^2}$$

### 7. Simple Interest

* **Basic Simple Interest & Amount:**

$$\text{SI} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}, \qquad \text{Amount} = P + \text{SI}$$


* **Reinvested Simple Interest:**

$$P_2 = P_1 + \text{SI}_1 \implies \text{Final Amount} = P_2 + \frac{P_2 \times R_2 \times T_2}{100}$$



---

### 8. Problems on Ages & Averages

* **Ratio-Difference Shortcut (Ages):** When the difference between the two ratio terms is equal at both time points, $\Delta\text{ ratio units} = \Delta\text{ years}$.
* **Sum Shift for $n$ People over $t$ Years:**

$$\text{Present Sum} = \text{Past Sum} + (n \times t)$$


* **Average of Newly Added Members:**

$$\text{Avg}_{\text{new}} = \frac{(N_{\text{total}} \times \text{Avg}_{\text{final}}) - (N_{\text{old}} \times \text{Avg}_{\text{old}})}{N_{\text{new}}}$$



---

### 9. Simplification & Arithmetic

* **Order of Operations (`BODMAS`):** Brackets $\rightarrow$ Of ($\times$) $\rightarrow$ Division ($\div$) $\rightarrow$ Multiplication ($\times$) $\rightarrow$ Addition ($+$) $\rightarrow$ Subtraction ($-$).
* **Percentage Conversion:** $x\% \text{ of } y = \frac{x}{100} \times y$.

---

### 10. Standalone Aptitude & Reasoning

* **Calendars (Same Date Across Years):**
* Ordinary year $= +1\text{ odd day}$
* Leap year (only if **29th February** falls between the two dates) $= +2\text{ odd days}$


* **Time & Work (Fraction Left after $d$ days):**

$$\text{Work Left} = 1 - d\left(\frac{1}{A} + \frac{1}{B}\right)$$


* **Probability (Mutually Exclusive Events):**

$$P(A \text{ or } B) = P(A) + P(B) = \frac{\text{Favourable Outcomes}}{\text{Total Outcomes}}$$


* **Number Series (Odd One Out):** Test for primes, perfect squares ($n^2$), perfect cubes ($n^3$), and numbers that are **both** a square and a cube ($n^6$, such as $64 = 4^3 = 8^2$).
